# Reelmeter
菲林测光表｜Reelmeter
专为胶片手动相机摄影师打造的随身测光工具。
老旧机械相机没有内置测光？出门不想额外携带笨重测光表？打开 FilmLume，手机立刻变成便携曝光测光仪。
【核心功能】
实时测光：平均测光、点测光，读取场景 EV 曝光值
曝光计算器：输入 ISO，自动给出全套光圈、快门组合
胶卷预设库：柯达、富士黑白 / 彩色负片、反转片，自定义胶片 ISO 与曝光偏移
互易律补偿：长曝光拍摄自动修正胶片互易失效
曝光补偿、区域曝光参考，兼顾扫街、人像、风光、中画幅大画幅创作
适配尼康 F2、宾得 67、哈苏 500CM、徕卡 M、奥林巴斯等全系列手动胶片相机。
不管是 35mm、120 中画幅胶片，黑白还是彩色胶片，都可以快速得到可靠曝光参考。
适合胶片新手学习曝光，也适合资深胶片摄影师外出扫街、外景拍摄快速测光，对焦。界面简洁干净，无多余广告，现场操作一步到位。

A portable light meter built exclusively for film photographers using manual cameras.

No built-in meter on your vintage mechanical camera? Tired of carrying a bulky standalone light meter? Launch FilmLume, and turn your phone into a compact exposure meter instantly.

## Core Features

• Real-time metering: Spot and evaluative metering to read scene EV values
• Exposure calculator: Input ISO to get a full set of aperture and shutter speed combinations automatically
• Film preset library: Kodak, Fujifilm B&W, color negative and slide films; custom ISO and exposure bias support
• Reciprocity compensation: Automatically correct for film reciprocity failure during long exposures
• Exposure compensation & Zone System reference. Great for street photography, portraiture, landscapes, medium and large format shooting.

Works with manual film cameras including Nikon F2, Pentax 67, Hasselblad 500CM, Leica M, Olympus and more.

Get reliable exposure readings quickly for 35mm, 120 medium format, black-and-white and color films alike.

Ideal for beginners learning exposure fundamentals, as well as seasoned film photographers for fast metering and focusing on street and outdoor shoots. Clean, ad-free interface for streamlined on-location operation.

Pick your exposure settings and focus distance. No need to peer into the viewfinder screen. Simply set your lens focus distance and exposure parameters to achieve results comparable to cameras with auto-exposure.
